# Boltzmann Machines

This research investigates the integration of physical constraints into Restricted Boltzmann Machines to develop Physics-Informed RBMs (PI-RBMs) for reconstructing solutions to partial differential equations. The core hypothesis suggests that the generative nature of PI-RBMs provides superior robustness and uncertainty quantification compared to standard Physics-Informed Neural Networks when reconstructing from sparse and noisy observations. The approach is evaluated through a comparative analysis of reconstruction accuracy and physical residuals across varying levels of data sparsity and noise.
